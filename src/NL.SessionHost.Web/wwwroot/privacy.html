Privacy Policy

Version 2026-08-01 · Terms of Service · Status

Data we process

NL processes Steam/platform identifiers for ownership verification, session metadata, moderation logs, and optional streamer registration details (display name, contact).

Retention

Moderation and compliance records are retained for at least 730 days unless a shorter period is required by law. Operators may export or delete SP profiles via GDPR endpoints when enabled.

Your rights

  • Export — POST /api/v1/fleet/compliance/export/{playerId} (operator)
  • Delete — DELETE /api/v1/fleet/compliance/sp/{playerId} (operator)

Security

Public endpoints use rate limiting and WebSocket connection caps. Production deployments require HTTPS and operator authentication for privileged APIs.

Backups

Fleet configuration and compliance stores are backed up on a schedule defined by the operator. Backup manifests are verified by the launch-ops validation gate.

Updates

Material changes to this policy will increment the legal version shown above and on the launch-ops settings API.
