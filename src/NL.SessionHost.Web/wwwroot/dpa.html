Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Version 2026-08-01 · Privacy Policy

Scope

This DPA applies when NexoraLive processes personal data on behalf of streamers and players under the hosted fleet program.

Processing activities

  • Steam/platform identity verification
  • Session and moderation logging
  • Streamer registration contact details
  • GDPR export and delete on operator request

Retention

Moderation and compliance records are retained for at least 730 days unless a shorter period is required by law.

Subprocessors

See the subprocessor list for third parties engaged in processing.
